# Motion v1

## Product Boundary

Motion v1 is the Cognitive Intelligence runtime for observed system reality.

The v1 pipeline is:

    Runtime Reality
        |
        v
    Ingestion
        |
        v
    Observation
        |
        v
    Perception
        |
        v
    Cognitive Intelligence
        |
        v
    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    Evaluation

Motion owns this pipeline through the production of two cognitive artifacts:

- `SystemAnalysis`
- `Evaluation`

These artifacts are the Motion v1 boundary.

## Cognitive Intelligence

The complete Cognitive Intelligence architecture contains four cognitive operations:

    Compare
        |
        v
    System Analysis
        |
        v
    Evaluate
        |
        v
    Learning

Motion v1 deliberately implements only:

    Perception
        |
        v
    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    Evaluation

`SystemAnalysis` consumes the current `Perception`.

`Evaluation` consumes the resulting `SystemAnalysis`.

The schema and internal design of these artifacts are defined during their implementation. This document locks the product boundary, not their internal representation.

## Deliberately Outside v1

The following cognitive operations are part of the larger Cognitive Intelligence architecture but are deliberately outside Motion v1:

- Compare
- Learning

Existing `HistoricalView`, `HistoricalViewWindow`, and `Compare` groundwork does not create a v1 completion dependency.

They remain available for later cognition work without expanding the Motion v1 feature boundary.

## SystemAnalysis

`SystemAnalysis` is analysis of observed system reality.

It is not:

- chat
- summarization
- interpretation
- reporting
- a dashboard
- an analytics engine

Motion produces the `SystemAnalysis` artifact.

`SystemAnalysis` is stored in `AnalysisStore`.

The boundary is:

    Motion
        |
        v
    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    AnalysisStore

Consumers of `AnalysisStore` are outside the Motion runtime.

## Analytics

Motion does not implement an analytics engine.

Analytics is a solved external product category.

The integration boundary is:

    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    AnalysisStore
        |
        v
    Analytics Engine

Motion v1 will support one analytics engine as the first official integration.

The current first-integration candidate is Apache Superset.

Superset is an integration choice, not part of Motion's Cognitive Intelligence architecture.

Additional analytics engines may consume the same boundary later without changing Motion.

## Evaluation

`Evaluation` is the second artifact produced by Motion Cognitive Intelligence.

The v1 cognition path is:

    Perception
        |
        v
    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    Evaluation

Motion ends at the `Evaluation` artifact.

Action and mutation begin outside Motion.

## Action Boundary

The downstream execution path is conceptually:

    Evaluation
        |
        v
    Act
        |
        v
    Loom
        |
        v
    Mutation

This path is outside Motion v1.

Motion v1 does not own:

- Act
- Loom invocation
- mutation execution
- AI governance around mutation
- mutation audit logging

Those capabilities consume Motion intelligence across a clean boundary.

## Dashboard Boundary

A future intelligence-facing dashboard may extend ConductorUI around LLM and intelligence transactions.

That is not generic observability and is not a Motion v1 dependency.

Motion will not build dashboard or analytics functionality merely because `SystemAnalysis` exists.

The `SystemAnalysis` dataset remains the product boundary.

Motion treats both as black boxes.

## Motion v1 Feature Lock

The Motion v1 product is:

    Runtime Reality
        |
        v
    Ingestion
        |
        v
    Observation
        |
        v
    Perception
        |
        v
    Cognitive Intelligence
        |
        +----> SystemAnalysis ----> AnalysisStore
        |
        +----> Evaluation

The Cognitive Intelligence execution path for v1 is:

    Perception
        |
        v
    SystemAnalysis
        |
        v
    Evaluation

The following are explicitly outside the v1 feature lock:

- Compare
- Learning
- analytics engine implementation
- dashboard implementation
- Act
- Loom invocation
- mutation
- AI governance around mutation
- mutation audit logging

Motion v1 produces intelligence.

Applications, analytics, and action consume it.

## Build Phase

The Motion v1 feature surface is locked.

Implementation now proceeds mechanically against this boundary.

New implementation work must complete the locked v1 surface rather than expand it.

The personal target is the third week of October 2026.

This is a target, not a reason to move the feature boundary or weaken an invariant.

Motion v1 is ready when the locked product is ready.
